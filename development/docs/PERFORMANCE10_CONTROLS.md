# Inputsignwave Performance 10 controls

By: Inputsignwave

## Engines and sounds

Choose PROPHET, FM6, the original WAVETABLE, WAVE LAYERS, DRUM (native 808/909),
or FM DRUM KIT. Original preset identities remain available; all approved new
sounds are additions. SID/CZ/SID kits and old ROOM/REVERB are retired.

WAVE LAYERS has eight families: Vowel Glass, Metallic PM, Hollow Motion, Prime
Spectrum, Rippling Digital, Phase Prism, Organic Voice and FM Spectral. It has
four-note polyphony; both layers belong to each note. ABNK/APOS and BBNK/BPOS
choose family and frame position. A/B balances the layers; MRPH/RATE/SYNC animate
positions, filter endpoints and balance. Layer pages contain independent level,
tuning, ADSR, cutoff, resonance, envelope depth, scan rate/depth/rise/endpoints,
sync and retrigger. Existing common modulation can also move frame position.
The two layers are audible when their levels and A/B balance allow them.

FM6 adds 25 voices while keeping INIT at its original index. FM DRUM KIT has
ELECTRONIC, METAL and TUNED presets. Its eight lanes follow kick, snare, clap,
closed hat, open hat, tom, rim and bell. Select a lane's patch and level to edit
it. A hit captures its native FM patch; editing another lane does not change a
ringing hit. These kits use native FM operators and envelopes with a short
percussion gate; individual FM6 presets retain their full melodic controls.

## Stereo plate and performance FX

Select PLATE on one of the three shared send buses and raise track sends.
Only one PLATE instance is supported; it is unavailable as a track insert.
The normal effect controls set SIZE and DECAY. Select the bus on the additional
FX parameter pages for predelay, tone, wet low/high cuts, damping, width,
movement/rate/sync, optional wobble, Swell/rise and Hold. Wet filters/tone do not
filter the dry signal. Size changes the delay network; it is not another mix dial.

Swell ducks/rises the wet return after a quiet gap. Hold closes new input and
sustains the captured tail; release Hold to resume normal input and decay.
Movement and wobble have neutral zero settings. This is the auditioned
Rings-derived plate adapted for FM-1, not a model of a commercial pedal.

WARM DRIVE adds softened/asymmetric saturation with drive, output and tone.
Every active effect slot has an independent modulation oscillator: shape,
destination (OFF/MIX/RATE/CHAR/TONE), depth, rate and SYNC. When synced, RATE
selects a division, including triplets and dotted values. The destination's
meaning follows that effect: PLATE RATE controls size and CHAR controls decay.

The FX-page slot selector numbers 1–12 address tracks 1–4, three inserts each;
13–15 address the three shared buses. Selecting the correct slot matters.
Plate movement timing is separate from the slot LFO.

## Saving and installation

Projects/full-bank exports and templates preserve new sound-FX controls. Older
bank/template versions load with neutral extension settings. Standalone legacy
FUN22 exports retain their historical scope and do not carry the new extension.
User presets preserve engine-layer/kit parameters through the existing common
parameter format. Back up a full bank before flashing; older firmware cannot
read new FBK8/TPLM saves. Heavy configurations can leave no room for the optional
recording-undo snapshot; the UI reports this while recording/audio take priority. NIGHT applies once on upgrade, then your palette choice
is retained. No flash operation is performed by the validation process.
